# Dolly Retroware 🐑

Dolly is intended to be distributed **free of charge**. No payment, donation or equipment is required to use it.

If Dolly is useful and you'd like to say thank you, the author welcomes *voluntary* retro-computing artifacts: whole Amigas, boards, cases, keyboards, floppy drives, accelerator cards, CPUs, memory, and other Amiga-compatible or interesting vintage computer parts — functional **or broken**.

“The sheep appreciate anything that belongs in an Amiga barn!”

Do **not** ship equipment without first contacting the project maintainer and agreeing on destination and shipping. No physical shipping address is published here.

A thank-you donation is not a product purchase, a license obligation, or a guarantee of service, features or support. The program is intended to remain usable without any gift.

**Code licensing is a separate decision and is not yet finalized.**
