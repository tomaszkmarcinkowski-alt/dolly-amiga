## Safety and limitations

- No destructive I/O implemented. No filesystem repair, no image restore, no Smart Clone.
- `dolly_amiga_source.c` is **experimental**: requires real NDK/68000 build, DMA mask and MaxTransfer verification, actual device capacity discovery and controller tests.
- NSD64 is explicitly checked; TD64 is not implemented.
- RDB parsing is preliminary: RDB reserved metadata and some MBR/GPT/hybrid layouts still unsupported.
- IFF acceptance means a valid outer envelope, **not** a complete or data-verified DLY archive.
- Embedded RDB arrays may exceed tiny Amiga task stacks; see issue #9.
